# Brand Discovery Worksheet

## חלק 1: מי אתם (project-brief)

### 1. במשפט אחד - מה אתם עושים?
We provide unbiased, commercial-intent B2B software reviews and interactive recommendation engines for professional services (law firms, CPAs, agencies, real estate, photographers, and coaches) to help them select and implement the right CRM, client portals, and onboarding workflows in under 60 seconds.

### 2. איזו בעיה אתם פותרים?
Solopreneurs and firm owners waste endless hours chasin client intake documents, dealing with messy communication across scattered tools, and trying to decipher generic software comparisons that don't address their specific vertical workflows (like tax intake, legal case tracking, or session booking).

### 3. מה הופך אתכם לשונים?
Interactive Decision Engine: We don't just write long text reviews; our interactive grid filters tools dynamically based on exact business models, goals, and budget constraints.

Vertical-Specific Depth: We focus strictly on BoFu (Bottom-of-Funnel) workflows—such as client document intake, tax season automation, and retainers—rather than shallow high-level sales software summaries.

Practical Implementation Specifications: Every software guide includes exact setup frameworks, Rank Math SEO parameters, and workflow integrations ready for immediate deployment.

---

## חלק 2: למי אתם מדברים (icp-profile)

### 4. תארו את הלקוח האידיאלי שלכם
Managing partners, solo practitioners, and founders of service-based businesses (specifically Law Firms, CPA/Accounting Practices, B2B Agencies, Real Estate Brokers, Photography Studios, and Executive Coaches) who are actively looking to upgrade their backend tech stack and buy software today.

### 5. מה הם רוצים להשיג?
To streamline client onboarding, automate document collection, eliminate missed follow-ups, and run a organized backend operation without spending extra time on software administration.

### 6. מה מפריע להם להגיע לשם?
Feature overload and paralysis by analysis. They've tried generic CRMs (like HubSpot or Salesforce) that required months of setup and still lacked vertical features like secure client document portals, legal billing, or intake forms.

---

## חלק 3: איך אתם נשמעים (voice-dna)

### 7. אם המותג שלכם היה אדם, איך הייתם מתארים אותו?
sharp, tech-savvy B2B operations partner who has tested every software on the market. Confident, direct, analytical, and practical—delivering actionable clarity with zero marketing hype or fluff.

### 8. מה אתם לא?
Never overly formal or dry corporate: We speak peer-to-peer like experienced tech advisors, not a stiff legacy enterprise agency.

Never salesy or pushy: We don't promote tools just because of affiliate payouts; recommendations are driven by features, pricing fairness, and setup utility.

Never vague or superficial: We never write high-level "fluff" articles without explicit pricing tiers, pros/cons, and vertical context.

### 9. תנו דוגמה למשפט שנשמע כמוכם
Generic CRMs track sales pipelines, but they won't collect tax files or sign engagement letters. Here is the exact tech stack built for your actual workflow.

### 10. מי ההשראות שלכם?
G2 / Capterra: For comprehensive structural software data and feature scoring (but streamlined for speed).

Marketing Examples (Harry Dry): For concise, high-converting visual tables and zero-fluff messaging.

NerdWallet / Wirecutter: For authoritative, trustworthy, independent review frameworks and clear affiliate disclosures.
