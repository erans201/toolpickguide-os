# 👥 04 · Who does what

## The robot and agents do on their own
- Data pulls and reports (Search Console, GA4, Bing, DataForSEO up to $5/month, speed).
- Junia briefs with verified prices; Junia drafts in your Chrome (drafts only).
- QA and fixes on **drafts**: prices checked against the facts sheet, SEO fields, links, images copied into the Media Library.
- Internal links on live pages (small, backed up, verified, auto-undo).
- Judging Junia's external links (trusted or blocked list).
- Quora thread finding and answer drafts.
- Search Console indexing requests (browser agent).
- Affiliate applications: filling forms and accepting standard terms (your standing instruction: "do not ask for permission, exert full control").
- Reading affiliate replies in the mailbox (only allowlisted senders) and adding approved links to page files.
- Emailing you the daily, weekly and monthly message.

## Only you (about 20–30 minutes a week)

| What | Why it stays with you |
|---|---|
| **Publishing** a draft (Save Draft → read → Publish) | your final check. You said yes to automating it (D1), but the agent's safety check blocked it |
| **Replacing a live page** (`--replace-live`) and other `--apply` commands | changes to live pages; the agent's safety check blocks it from running them |
| **Quora/Reddit "Post"** click (or your "post 1, 3" OK) | public content under your name |
| Accounts, passwords, login codes, captchas | the agent never enters or creates these |
| Payment and tax forms (PayPal, W-8BEN) | personal financial data |
| Approving any email the agent sends for you (reply "send") | it goes out in your name |
| DataForSEO spending above $5/month | your money |
| Editing the Weekly routine's text | the agent's edits to that routine get blocked |

## Nobody ever
- Permanently deletes anything (only Trash, with a backup).
- Changes the theme or layout, installs or deletes plugins, or touches users, security or payment settings.
- Puts a password or key in chat, a document, or GitHub.
- Publishes a page that competes with an existing page for the same search.
- Posts on Quora/Reddit without your OK for that specific answer.

## The agent may change (with you present)
- LiteSpeed Cache and Rank Math settings: one at a time, after your OK, logged in `wordpress/SETTINGS-LOG.md` with the undo.
