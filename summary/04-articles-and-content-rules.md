# 04 · Articles and content rules (2026-10-19)

## From idea to live page
1. **Topic** from the content queue; must pass the topic gate and `cannibal_check.py` (no two pages for the same search).
2. **Brief** with verified prices (weekly robot, Monday). No FAQ in the brief: Junia writes its own.
3. **Writer:** Junia (browser robot, Tue/Fri) or our own robot (cloud, Tue/Fri). Both end as a **draft**.
4. **QA:** every number checked against the brief, and **every image looked at** (garbled text or unrelated pictures get swapped first, rule of Oct 14).
5. **Publish** by the cloud robot in the morning (since Oct 17) or the PC robot at 20:00, only if the check is clean.
6. **Indexing** requested; **internal links** added.

Existing live pages are upgraded in-house; the PC robot runs the replace.

## The content test (started Oct 9)
- **J** = Junia writes, **C** = our robot writes. Each Monday: 8 topics in 4 same-type pairs, coin flip decides who writes which.
- Publishing weeks **Oct 12 – Nov 22**. Interim look **Nov 23**, verdict **Jan 4, 2027**.
- Judged by Google reach (Search Console impressions at day 42) **and** reader behavior (GA4 engagement, Clarity scroll and heatmaps).
- **Week 1 (3 pairs):** service contract template (C) vs proposal template (J) · Clio vs MyCase (C) vs Follow Up Boss vs Lofty (J) · Lawmatics review (J, Junia draft due) vs Capsule CRM review (C). 5 of 6 live.
- **Week 2 (3 pairs, Oct 19):** client portal software (C) vs Dubsado alternatives (J) · graphic design contract template (J) vs client onboarding checklist (C) · MyCase review (C) vs Paperbell review (J).
- **Left out:** Clio Grow (8,100 searches, but clio.com blocks the robot, so no verified prices) · Smokeball review (no public prices) · HoneyBook vs Dubsado (no same-type partner; next week) · invoicing/scheduling software (not in the approved topics yet).

## Junia settings
2,700 words · images, **FAQ** and external links ON · **Mode = draft** · fill the brief field by field.

## Writing rules
- Bold keyword hook → quick answer near the top → body → "don't mess this up" tips → 5–6 FAQs (one FAQ section).
- Max 2–3 sentences per paragraph; friendly expert voice.
- Banned: delve, testament, furthermore, in conclusion, unlock, game-changer, seamless, "in today's fast-paced world".
- Never "tested" or "hands-on". No invented numbers.

## Facts, SEO, links
- Every price comes from the vendor's official page, with a date and a **Source:** link.
- Focus keyword in title, first paragraph, a subheading, slug and meta description; correct year.
- Internal links only to live pages (the robot unlinks others); trusted outbound links; affiliate links `sponsored nofollow` with the disclosure box.

## Images
- **Normal stock photos only** (Unsplash style: real people, desks, offices). No AI images, no illustrations.
- Photos are copied into the Media Library; keyword in the alt text.
