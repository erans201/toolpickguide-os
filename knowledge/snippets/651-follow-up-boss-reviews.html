What Follow Up Boss users praise

  • Easy to learn: reviewers keep saying new agents pick it up quickly, and the mobile app gets regular praise.
  • Lead routing that just works: leads from portals like Zillow, Realtor.com and Facebook are assigned and tracked automatically.
  • Team accountability: team leaders like seeing who followed up, as long as agents actually log their calls.
  • Open connections: users connect their own web forms and trigger action plans from them.

What Follow Up Boss users complain about

  • Price adds up: the most common complaint is cost for solo agents and small teams, especially as you add users.
  • Automation gaps: some want more built-in automation, such as batch texting or automatic newsletters.
  • App hiccups: a few reviewers report mobile app glitches with basic tasks like logging calls, and slow fixes.

Treat these as patterns from a few dozen reviews, not a verdict. If price is your main worry, run the year-one scenarios above with your real team size before you decide.
