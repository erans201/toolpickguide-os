## Clio Grow features (https://www.clio.com/grow/, checked October 19, 2026)

- Positioning: "Always on client acquisition for law firms"; client intake and CRM for law firms.
- Custom intake forms shared on the website, by email or text; data syncs to Clio Manage when a lead becomes a client.
- Document drafting from templates (engagement letters, retainers), auto-filled from intake data.
- E-signature for clients from any device.
- "Grow AI": answers the phone and emails after hours, screens leads for fit and conflicts, books qualified prospects into appointments, nurtures them after the case closes.
- Online scheduling for consultations (also on the pricing page: "Book consultations and run intake with online forms and scheduling").
- Email marketing; website building ("Build your presence with a professional website").
