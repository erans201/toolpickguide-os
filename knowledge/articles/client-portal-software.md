**Client portal software** gives every client one login for files, messages, invoices and status updates, so you stop digging through email threads. That's the promise, anyway.

The problem it solves is painfully familiar. A contract sits in one inbox, the latest draft in another, and a client emails "where are we on this?" for the third time this week.

Nobody did anything wrong. There was just no single place to look.

The catch is pricing. These tools charge in completely different ways: one flat price, per user, per active client, or per builder. This guide ranks 7 tools for small service businesses, with prices taken from each vendor's own pricing page, and shows which one fits which kind of business.

## What a client portal for small business should actually do

Think of a client portal as the front desk of your business, open 24/7. Clients walk in, grab what they need, leave a note and go. You don't have to be there.

A useful client portal for small business covers seven jobs:

- **Files:** one place for contracts, drafts, deliverables and anything the client uploads.
- **Messages:** conversations tied to the client or project, not buried in your inbox.
- **Invoices and payments:** clients see what they owe and pay without asking for a new link.
- **Forms:** questionnaires and intake forms the client fills in once. If you need a starting point, use our [client intake form template](/client-intake-form-template/).
- **Tasks and status:** what's done, what's next, who's waiting on whom.
- **Branding:** your logo and, ideally, your own domain, so it feels like your business.
- **Permissions and logins:** each client sees only their own stuff.

Skip two or three of these and you're back in email for the missing parts. Duct tape, with a nicer login screen.

## Secure client portal checklist: 6 questions before you buy

A secure client portal is less about a padlock icon and more about boring controls. Ask these six questions before you put client files in any tool:

1. **Who can see what?** Look for per-client permissions. Assembly Advanced lists client access permissions; Clinked Premium and Softr Pro list role-based or granular permissions.
2. **Is there two-factor or MFA?** Softr Business includes 2FA, Assembly Advanced enforces MFA and Clinked Premium can enforce 2FA.
3. **Can you use your own domain?** Clinked Standard, Assembly Professional, Zendo Pro and Softr Basic list it.
4. **Is there an audit trail?** Clinked Standard has an audit trail and Assembly Advanced an audit log.
5. **Where does data live, and is it encrypted?** Zendo states encryption at rest and in transit; Clinked Enterprise lets you choose a US, EU or RoW data center.
6. **Do you need a HIPAA BAA?** Among these plans, Assembly Advanced is the one whose pricing page lists HIPAA compliance with a BAA.

If a vendor's page doesn't mention a control, assume it's missing until they confirm it in writing.

## Don't mess this up: 5 tips before you pick a portal

1. **Price your real client count, not today's.** Assembly and Softr cap the people who can log in, so a busy quarter can push you up a tier. Count where you'll be in a year.
2. **Check who's counted.** Some tools count clients, some count staff, some count builders. Softr counts anyone outside your business email domain as a client user.
3. **Read what "unlimited" covers.** SuiteDash's unlimited clients and staff is real, but storage still has a cap per plan.
4. **Trial with one real client before migrating.** Invite one friendly client and run a full cycle: file, message, invoice. Then move the rest.
5. **Confirm branding is on the plan you buy.** Custom domains and badge removal often sit one tier above the entry plan (Assembly Professional, Zendo Pro, Softr Pro).

If you do nothing else, do tip 4. The best client portal software is the one your clients actually open, not the one with the longest feature list.

## FAQs About Client Portal Software

### What is client portal software?

It's a secure, branded login where each client finds their files, messages, invoices and project status. Instead of hunting through email, they check one place, and so do you.

### What is the cheapest client portal software?

Several tools have a free plan: Assembly (5 active contacts), Zendo Essential (3 clients) and Softr Free (5 app users). Among paid entry plans, SuiteDash Start is $19/mo billed monthly and Softr Basic is $19/mo billed yearly.

### Which client portal has unlimited clients?

SuiteDash includes unlimited clients and staff on every plan. HoneyBook and Dubsado list unlimited clients and projects, and Zendo Core and up list unlimited clients.

### Is a client portal secure?

It depends on the plan. Look for per-client permissions, MFA or 2FA, and an audit trail; for example, Assembly Advanced ($499/mo billed annually) includes an audit log, enforced MFA and a HIPAA BAA.

### Do I need a separate CRM if I buy a client portal?

Not always. SuiteDash, HoneyBook, Dubsado and Assembly include CRM features, while Clinked and Softr are mainly portals, so a dedicated CRM can sit in front of them.

### Can I use my own domain for a client portal?

Yes, on the right plan. Custom domains are listed on Assembly Professional ($99/mo billed annually), Zendo Pro ($49/mo billed annually), Softr Basic ($19/mo billed yearly) and Clinked Standard ($239/mo billed yearly).
