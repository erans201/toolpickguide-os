# 📏 06 · Content rules (what every page must follow)

## Writing
- **Length (new in-house articles only):** comparison/pricing pages 2,500–3,000 words; guides 1,800–2,500. Not a limit for Junia drafts or upgrades.
- **Shape:** bold keyword hook in line 1 → quick answer/ranking near the top → body → "don't mess this up" tips → 5–6 FAQs (one FAQ section only).
- **Style:** max 2–3 sentences per paragraph; direct, friendly expert; analogies over spec sheets. Voice sample: `brand-voice-sample.md`.
- **Banned words:** delve · testament · furthermore · in conclusion · in today's fast-paced world · unlock · game-changer · seamless.
- **Honesty:** never "tested", "hands-on" or "in our testing". No invented statistics, laws, ratings or prices.

## Facts and prices
- Every price and limit comes from the vendor's **official pricing page**, with the check date ("Pricing verified YYYY-MM-DD" shows on the page).
- A `**Source:**` link to the official pricing page sits under each tool's pricing.
- Junia may use **only** numbers from its brief's facts sheet. QA checks every number.

## SEO
- Focus keyword in: title, first paragraph, one subheading, slug, meta description.
- Correct current year in titles; numbers in titles ("60 questions") must match the content.
- **No cannibalization:** before any new page, check the full sitemap and `VERTICAL_MATRIX.md`. If a live page already serves the search, improve that page instead.
- Slugs: flat, no year (`/best-crm-for-law-firms/`).

## Links
- Required internal links to related live pages.
- Outbound links to trusted sources (official pricing pages, government, trade press); every URL checked before adding.
- Affiliate links get `rel="sponsored nofollow"` automatically; citation links stay followed.
- Every page with affiliate links has a disclosure that links to the FTC guidance.
- Junia's own external links: fine when the site is trusted; the agent judges each new domain once (`knowledge/junia-link-domains.txt`).

## Images
- **Images we make ourselves:** realistic, photo style. No overlaid text, logos or brand names. Text that's naturally on items (a screen, a notebook) is fine.
- **Junia's images:** generally safe (decision 2026-10-05). They may show text and logos. Flag one only if it's broken, offensive or unrelated.
- Junia's stock photos are copied into the Media Library automatically (no hotlinking).
- Keyword in the featured image's alt text.
