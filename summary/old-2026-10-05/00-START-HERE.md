## The 5 things most worth remembering

1. **The robot runs the site's routine work every day** and emails you a "Message for you" (from eran@toolpickguide.com to cezaris.joe@gmail.com). Its title says OK, Needs you, or ALERT.
2. **You stay in charge of anything public or permanent:** publishing, replacing live pages, Quora "Post" clicks, accounts, passwords, captchas and payments.
3. **Emergency stop:** create a file named `AUTOPILOT_PAUSED` in the GitHub repo (steps in file 03). Every robot stops.
4. **Never share secret files** (`.env`, `gsc-key.json`) in chat or anywhere else (file 14).
5. **Money comes from affiliate links on comparison pages.** Paperbell, Simply.Coach and Close are approved; more are pending (file 08).
