## 📋 FACTS SHEET (the only numbers and claims allowed)

All fetched from official vendor pages on **2026-10-05**.

**HoneyBook plans** (https://www.honeybook.com/pricing)
| Plan | Annual billing (per month) | Monthly billing | Includes / limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $29/mo | $36/mo | Unlimited clients and projects · invoices and payments · proposals and contracts · calendar · all templates · client portal · basic reports · up to 2 live lead forms · HoneyBook AI · scheduler: 1 session, email-only reminders |
| Essentials ("Most Popular") | $49/mo | $59/mo | Everything in Starter + scheduler (unlimited sessions, email and SMS reminders) · automations · QuickBooks Online integration · up to 2 team members · up to 10 live lead forms · remove "Powered by HoneyBook" · standard reports |
| Premium | $109/mo | $129/mo | Everything in Essentials + unlimited team members · priority support · multiple companies · advanced reports · unlimited live lead forms · onboarding specialist (1:1 setup in the first 90 days) |
- Annual toggle reads "Billed yearly (save up to 18%)". "Plans start as low as $29/mo."
- Unlimited clients and projects on every plan. Free file setup: HoneyBook's team turns your existing documents into templates.
- No paid seat add-ons are listed on the pricing page.
- Referral sign-ups "get a discount" (no amount stated → do not give a number). Promo codes apply at checkout (no amount → do not give a number).

**Trial and guarantee**
- Free for 30 days, no credit card required; "minor limitations based on eligibility for some advanced features" (honeybook.com/pricing FAQ).
- The trial does not roll into a paid plan automatically (help.honeybook.com/en/articles/7020217-top-questions-answered-about-honeybook-billing-and-membership).
- 60-day money-back guarantee: full refund if not happy after the first 60 days (honeybook.com/pricing).

**Payment processing fees** (honeybook.com/pricing unless noted)
- Cards: "Card fees start at 2.7% + 10¢" (processing-fees section and FAQ). **The same page's plan comparison table says "Fees starting at 2.9% + 25¢".** Article must mention both and tell readers to confirm in their account.
- No international or premium card brand fees; no extra fees for international clients (help.honeybook.com/en/articles/2209074-accepted-credit-cards-and-banks-for-client-payments).
- ACH bank transfer: 1.5%.
- Discounted card fees possible above $500,000 a year in transactions on Premium.
- Instant deposit: 1% fee, "pending eligibility".
- Dispute fee: $10 (charged by Stripe) (help.honeybook.com/en/articles/9472957-glossary-finance-and-payments).
