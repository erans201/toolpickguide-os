1. **Scheduling & Task Assignment (PM Agent):** The Project Manager maps out the weekly autonomous schedule blocks for all sub-agents and logs the master tracking arrays inside `INDEX.md` and `LOOP.md`.
2. **The QA Verification Gate (QA Sub-Agent):** No sub-agent task can be finalized or logged into our central tracking files until the QA Gatekeeper reviews its output, verifies technical compliance against `MANDATE.md`, and signs off.
3. **Execution Tracks (SEO, Onsite, Affiliate, Marketing):**
   - **SEO Agent** dumps keyword matrices and competitor gaps into `MEMORY.md`.
   - **Affiliate Agent** logs vendor signup states and link strings into `MEMORY.md`.
   - **Onsite Agent** compiles page/metadata optimization plans into the database.
   - **Marketing Agent** outlines community engagement campaigns for Reddit/Quora.
4. **Google Sheets Progress Syncing (PM Agent):** Once the QA Sub-Agent approves an execution run, the PM Agent formats the raw milestone into a row-delimited dataset prepared for clean Google Sheets integration.
