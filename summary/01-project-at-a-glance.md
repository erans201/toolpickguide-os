# 🌐 01 · Project at a glance

## What the site is
- **Site:** https://toolpickguide.com ("Tool Pick Guide"), WordPress on Hostinger, Blocksy theme, Rank Math SEO, LiteSpeed Cache.
- **What it does:** independent software comparisons ("best X", "X vs Y", pricing guides, templates) for **small service businesses**.
- **How it earns:** affiliate commissions when readers sign up for a tool through our links. Rankings never change because of commissions.
- **Honesty rule:** every price links to the vendor's official pricing page with a date. We never claim "tested" or "hands-on" unless it really happened.
