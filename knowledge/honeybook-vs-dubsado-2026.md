# HoneyBook vs Dubsado (2026): The Honest Head-to-Head

**HoneyBook vs Dubsado** is the most common fork in the road for photographers, designers, planners, and consultants, and in 2026 the gap between them is wider than it looks. Client software used to be… kinda boring. Pick one, send a contract, move on.

These two made it a personality test.

HoneyBook is a nice rental apartment. Furnished, clean, move in this weekend. You'll pay more rent, and you can't knock down walls.

Dubsado is a house you build yourself. Cheaper per year, and you can put a wall anywhere. You'll also spend your weekends holding a level.

Neither is "better." One of them is better **for you**, and this page helps you figure out which in about five minutes.

## QUICK VERDICT (IF YOU JUST WANT THE ANSWER)

- **Choose HoneyBook** if you want to look polished this week, you hate setup, and your clients live on their phones.
- **Choose Dubsado** if you want deep automation, lower yearly cost, and you'll invest real setup time (or pay someone to).
- **Choose neither** if you're a coach selling packages (look at Paperbell), or a photographer who wants galleries in the same tool (look at Sprout Studio or Pixieset).

## Pricing: what you'll actually pay

### HoneyBook
- **Starter:** $29/month billed annually (about $36 monthly). 2 lead forms, 1 scheduler session type, email reminders only, no automations
- **Essentials:** $49/month billed annually ($59 monthly). Scheduler, automations, QuickBooks Online, 2 team members, 10 lead forms, SMS reminders
- **Premium:** $109/month billed annually ($129 monthly). Unlimited lead forms and team members
- **Payment fees:** cards from 2.7% + 10¢, ACH 1.5%
- **Source:** [HoneyBook's official pricing page](https://www.honeybook.com/pricing) (checked September 2026)

HoneyBook restructured its plans in February 2025. Pricing trackers report Starter's monthly rate roughly doubling. If you're on monthly billing, switching to annual is the fastest saving available.

### Dubsado
- **Starter:** $335/year. 1 lead form, invoicing, payment plans, client portal, form templates
- **Premier:** $525/year. Adds scheduling, automated workflows, public proposals, bookkeeping integration, Zapier
- **Add-ons:** extra brand $10/month; team users from $25/month (4–10 users)
- **Source:** [Dubsado's official pricing page](https://www.dubsado.com/pricing) (checked September 2026)

### The honest math

| Setup | HoneyBook | Dubsado |
|---|---|---|

On paper Dubsado wins by $63 a year. In practice the bigger number is **card fees**: on $60,000 of card payments, HoneyBook's 2.7% is $1,620 before the subscription. Dubsado runs payments through your connected processor, so compare that processor's rate, not just the subscription.

### Three real-world cost scenarios (year one)

**The new photographer** (a few shoots a month, needs contracts and invoices):
- HoneyBook Starter: $348/year, but no automations and one scheduler session type
- Dubsado Starter: $335/year, but no scheduling or workflows at all
- Honest take: at this stage, the free Pixieset Studio Manager plan may beat both.

**The busy wedding photographer** (~$80,000 a year, much of it by card):
- HoneyBook Essentials: $588/year + card fees (2.7% + 10¢ on every card payment)
- Dubsado Premier: $525/year + your processor's rates
- Honest take: the fee line matters more than the subscription line. Push balances to bank transfer either way.

**The two-brand studio** (weddings + portraits, one assistant):
- HoneyBook: plan around its team limits (2 members on Essentials) and check how it handles a second brand
- Dubsado Premier: $525/year + $120/year for the second brand
- Honest take: Dubsado's add-on pricing is built for this setup.

## Setup and learning curve

This is the real decision.

**HoneyBook** is built to get you live fast. Templates are solid out of the box, and you can send a professional proposal on day one.

**Dubsado** is built to be shaped. Forms, proposals, and workflows can branch on almost anything. That flexibility is the product, and it's also the cost.

A badly built Dubsado is worse than a default HoneyBook. A well-built Dubsado is better than anything HoneyBook can do. Which one you'll end up with depends on your patience, or your budget for a specialist.

**Rule of thumb:** if you've never built an automation in anything, start on HoneyBook or budget for Dubsado help.

## Proposals and client experience

HoneyBook's proposals are its crown jewel: packages, contract, and payment in one smooth scroll that feels like a boutique checkout. Clients rarely need help.

Dubsado's proposals can do the same, and its workflows can route clients differently depending on what they choose. Out of the box they look plainer. It takes design effort to match HoneyBook's polish.

**Test it from the client's side.** Send yourself a proposal from each trial and complete it on your phone. The one you'd rather pay through wins this round.

## Contracts, forms, and mobile

**Contracts.** Both include contracts with e-signatures on every plan. HoneyBook's templates are quicker to adapt. Dubsado's forms and contracts can pull in answers from earlier forms, so details like dates and package names fill themselves in once you've set it up.

**Forms.** HoneyBook caps live lead forms by plan: 2 on Starter, 10 on Essentials, unlimited on Premium. Dubsado Starter allows 1 active lead capture form, and Premier is unlimited. If you run separate inquiry forms per service, count them before you pick a tier.

**Mobile.** Both have mobile apps for managing clients on the go. The part that matters more is the client's side: signing and paying on a phone.

Test that in both trials. It's where bookings are won or lost.

## Automations

**HoneyBook** automations (Essentials and up) cover the common path: send this, wait, remind, move to the next step. They're easy to set up and easy to trust.

**Dubsado** workflows (Premier) go much deeper. Trigger actions on form answers, payment status, or dates.

Run different paths for different packages, and automate almost the entire client journey.

If "I wish it could do X when Y happens" is a sentence you say often, Dubsado is your tool.

## Scheduling

Both include a scheduler on the mid-tier: HoneyBook Essentials and Dubsado Premier. Neither Starter plan is a real scheduling solution.

HoneyBook's Starter allows **one** scheduler session type. Dubsado Starter has no scheduling at all. If clients book calls or sessions with you, budget for the middle tier on either side.

## Payments

HoneyBook processes payments itself: cards from 2.7% + 10¢, ACH at 1.5%. Simple, but you pay HoneyBook's rate.

Dubsado connects to a separate payment processor, so your cost depends on which one you use. Both support payment plans for installment-heavy work like weddings.

**Money move on either platform:** steer big balances toward bank transfer. The fee difference on a $5,000 invoice is about $60.

## Teams and multiple brands

- **HoneyBook Essentials** includes 2 team members. Premium removes the cap.
- **Dubsado** charges for team users as an add-on (from $25/month for 4–10 users) and extra brands at $10/month each.

Running two brands (say, weddings and portraits)? Dubsado adds extra brands for $10/month each. Check how HoneyBook handles a second brand before you commit.

## What neither tool does well

Knowing the gaps saves you a bad migration.

- **Client galleries.** Neither hosts them. Photographers still need Pixieset, Pic-Time, ShootProof, or an all-in-one like Sprout Studio.
- **Coaching programs.** Session packages, progress tracking, and group programs are bolt-ons at best. Coaches get more from purpose-built platforms.
- **Real accounting.** Both send invoices and connect to bookkeeping software on higher tiers, but neither replaces your accountant's system.
- **Selling products.** Prints, albums, and digital downloads belong in a store or gallery platform.

## Getting help

**HoneyBook** leans on polished templates and a guided setup, and its big user community means most "how do I…" questions already have an answer somewhere.

**Dubsado** has a whole ecosystem of independent setup specialists who build accounts for you. If you choose Dubsado but not the setup work, that's the realistic path. Get a quote before your trial ends so you can compare the true cost.

## Which one fits your industry?

### Photographers
Both handle inquiries, contracts, retainers, and payment plans, and **neither hosts galleries**, so you'll pair either one with Pixieset, Pic-Time, or ShootProof.

HoneyBook wins if your bookings are straightforward and you want to look polished fast. Dubsado wins if you run complex wedding timelines: questionnaires at six and three weeks out, different workflows for elopements vs full-day weddings, and payment schedules that end before the event.

If you'd rather have galleries in the same login, neither is your best pick. See our [photographer rankings](/client-management-software-for-photographers/).

### Coaches and consultants
Coaches sell packages and recurring sessions, not one-off proposals. Both tools can be bent into that shape, but neither is built for it.

Dubsado handles recurring work better thanks to deeper workflows. HoneyBook is simpler for consultants who send a proposal and then an invoice.

If sessions and packages are your whole business, a coaching platform fits better. See our [coaching platform comparison](/client-management-software-for-coaches/).

### Designers, planners, and agencies
This is home turf for both. Designers and planners who live on proposals and milestone payments love HoneyBook's polish.

Small agencies and multi-service studios that need different paths per service tend to outgrow it and land on Dubsado. Multiple brands tip it further toward Dubsado.

## Who should pick HoneyBook

- You want to be live **this week**
- Your clients are mostly on phones, and polish wins you bookings
- You have 2 people or fewer, and automations like "remind after 3 days" are enough
- You'd rather pay a bit more than spend weekends configuring software

## Who should pick Dubsado

- You want the client journey to **run itself**, including branches and conditions
- You're price-sensitive on the yearly bill
- You enjoy building systems, or you'll pay a specialist once
- You run multiple brands or packages with different paths

## Three myths worth ignoring

**"Dubsado is only for tech people."**
Not true. It's for patient people. The interface isn't hard; building good workflows just takes time and a clear map of your process. Sketch your client journey on paper first and Dubsado gets much easier.

**"HoneyBook can't automate anything."**
Also not true. Essentials handles the everyday path well: send a questionnaire, wait, remind, move the project forward. It just stops short of Dubsado's branching logic.

**"The cheaper sticker is the cheaper tool."**
Rarely. Your real cost is subscription plus payment fees plus setup hours. A $63-a-year saving disappears fast if Dubsado takes you 20 evenings to set up, or if card fees dwarf the subscription on either side.

## Switching between them

### From HoneyBook to Dubsado
1. **Export first.** Download your clients and projects from HoneyBook before your plan ends.
2. **Rebuild one path, not ten.** One lead form, one proposal, one contract, one workflow. Launch that.
3. **Run a fake client** through the whole thing on your phone.
4. **Send new inquiries to Dubsado.** Let active HoneyBook projects finish where they started.
5. **Add workflows in month two,** once real clients have shown you where the friction is.

### From Dubsado to HoneyBook
It usually happens when the automations became a second job. Keep your Dubsado account through your active projects, start new inquiries in HoneyBook on its default templates, and resist rebuilding every Dubsado branch. Simplicity is why you're switching.

## The three-question decision test

Answer honestly:

1. **Would you rather spend $60 more a year or 20 more hours setting up?** More money → HoneyBook. More hours → Dubsado.
2. **Do your clients take different paths depending on what they buy?** Yes → Dubsado. Mostly the same path → HoneyBook.
3. **Have you ever enjoyed building a spreadsheet formula?** Yes → Dubsado. That question isn't a joke.

Two or three answers pointing the same way? That's your tool.

## A few "don't mess this up" tips (from what goes wrong in real practices)

**Don't judge Dubsado by its default templates.**
Out of the box it looks plain. That's the blank canvas, not the ceiling.

**Don't pick HoneyBook Starter to save money.**
No automations and one scheduler session type. Most working businesses end up on Essentials within months, so price that from day one.

**Don't start your Dubsado trial on a busy week.**
Twenty-one days sounds like plenty. It isn't if you're shooting three weddings. Start it when you have evenings free.

**Don't forget the client's side.**
Complete a full proposal, contract, and payment on your phone in both trials before you decide.

**Don't switch mid-season.**
Whichever way you move, let active projects finish where they started and send new inquiries to the new tool.

## FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Is Dubsado cheaper than HoneyBook?
Per year, yes. Dubsado Premier is $525/year, and HoneyBook Essentials is $588/year on annual billing. Also compare payment processing fees: HoneyBook cards start at 2.7% + 10¢.

### Which is easier to use, HoneyBook or Dubsado?
HoneyBook. It's built to get you live fast with solid default templates. Dubsado is more powerful but takes weeks to set up well.

### Does HoneyBook or Dubsado have galleries?
Neither hosts client photo galleries. Photographers pair them with a gallery tool, or choose an all-in-one like Sprout Studio or Pixieset.

### Can Dubsado do everything HoneyBook does?
Nearly, and more on the automation side. The difference is effort: HoneyBook's polished client experience comes by default, while Dubsado's takes design and setup work.

### Is HoneyBook or Dubsado better for photographers?
HoneyBook suits photographers who want polish fast. Dubsado suits photographers with complex packages, payment plans, or multiple brands who'll invest in setup.

### Did HoneyBook raise its prices?
Yes. HoneyBook restructured its plans in February 2025. Current annual-billing prices are $29, $49, and $109 per month for Starter, Essentials, and Premium.
