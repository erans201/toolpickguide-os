## How the money adds up
**Clicks × share that click an affiliate link (~10–12%) × share that buy (2–5%) × average commission (~$60–100).**
At 1,500 clicks a month that's about 4–7 sales, $250–650/month. Strong comparison tables and high-value programs (like Lawmatics) could double it.

## Biggest levers, in order
1. Join programs and put the links on pages.
2. Rank the legal and real estate buying pages (most searches, highest value).
3. Conversion: comparison tables, "best for" boxes, clear buttons.
4. More programs per page, with honest rankings.
