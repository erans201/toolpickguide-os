# 04 · Articles and content rules

## From idea to live page
1. **Topic** from the content queue; must not compete with an existing page (sitemap check).
2. **Brief** with verified prices (weekly robot, Monday).
3. **Junia** writes it and saves a **draft** (browser helper, Tue/Fri).
4. **QA**: every number checked against the brief; fixes applied (daily robot).
5. **You publish** (about 1 minute).
6. **Indexing** requested; **internal links** added.

Live pages that already exist are upgraded in-house through their upgrade file, and you run `--replace-live`.

## Junia must-knows
- Fill the brief **field by field**, never one big paste.
- 2,700 words · competitor research OFF · Junia FAQ OFF · images ON · external links ON.
- **Mode = draft** when sending to WordPress. The default is public!

## Writing rules
- Bold keyword hook → quick answer near the top → body → "don't mess this up" tips → 5–6 FAQs.
- Max 2–3 sentences per paragraph; friendly expert voice.
- Banned: delve, testament, furthermore, in conclusion, unlock, game-changer, seamless, "in today's fast-paced world".
- Never "tested" or "hands-on". No invented numbers.

## Facts, SEO, links
- Every price comes from the vendor's official page, with a date and a **Source:** link.
- Focus keyword in title, first paragraph, a subheading, slug and meta description; correct year.
- Internal links to related pages; trusted outbound links; affiliate links marked `sponsored nofollow`; FTC disclosure.

## Images
- Ours: realistic photos, no overlaid text or logos (text naturally on items is fine).
- Junia's: generally safe, text and logos allowed; flag only broken, offensive or unrelated ones.
- Stock photos are copied into the Media Library; keyword in the alt text.
