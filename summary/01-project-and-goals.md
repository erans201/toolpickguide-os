# 01 · Project and goals

**ToolPickGuide.com** compares software for small service businesses and earns affiliate commissions when readers sign up through our links. Rankings never change because of commissions, and every price links to the vendor's official pricing page.

## Who it's for (priority order, decided 2026-10-02)
| # | Vertical | Google searches/month |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal | ~14,000 |
| 2 | Real estate | ~11,000 |
| 3 | Photographers | ~4,700 |
| 4 | Marketing agencies | ~1,400 |
| 5–6 | Accounting, coaches | maintain only |

Topic: client-operations software (CRM, intake, onboarding, contracts, e-sign). The old home-office posts are hidden from Google.

## Where we are (2026-10-05)
- About **22 live articles**; Google traffic still tiny (normal for a new site: 4–8 months).
- **$0 revenue** so far; first affiliate links live since Oct 5.
- Robots live since Oct 5; speed score up from 85 to 93.
